# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Personal job seeker needing a fast, efficient way to log job postings, compare them against their CV, and track required skill gaps over time. They use this in a focused workflow.

## Product Purpose
A dashboard and analyzer that ingests job descriptions via web or Telegram, parses them using an LLM to find CV matches and gaps, and presents a clear, actionable summary. Success looks like reducing the cognitive load of evaluating job fit to seconds per job.

## Brand Personality
Focused, utilitarian, fast. The design is clean, functional, and visually crisp—feeling more like a sharp, modern developer tool than a generic enterprise dashboard.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered SaaS templates
- Corporate enterprise software with excessive padding
- Heavy, slow-loading pages with unnecessary decoration

## Design Principles
- **Function over decoration**: Every visual element must serve the data.
- **Fast scanning**: The job gap analysis should be instantly readable through clear textual hierarchy.
- **Crisp and professional**: Even though it's utilitarian, the craft must be high-quality and look good.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No strict compliance requirements needed for this personal tool, but baseline functional contrast and basic readable text sizes apply.
